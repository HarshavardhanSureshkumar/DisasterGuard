Smart Disaster Response

The Problem

During disasters and emergencies, delayed communication and poor coordination can slow down rescue operations and put lives at risk.

Our Solution

DisasterGuard connects citizens, control centers, and rescue teams to enable instant SOS alerts, smart rescue allocation, real-time updates, and coordinated emergency response.

Key Features

Instant SOS Alerts

Send emergency requests with your location and essential incident details.

Smart Rescue Coordination

Control centers can monitor emergencies and assign the right rescue team quickly.

Real-Time Rescue Updates

Track emergency response from SOS submission to rescue completion.
